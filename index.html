Our Legacy

Rooted in the Soil of Pampore

At KesarHarvesters, saffron is not just a commercial product; it is a generational inheritance. Located in Namblabal, Pampore, our family has cultivated this sacred crop for four generations, protecting the ancient agricultural methods of the Kashmir Valley.

By cutting out B2B brokers and wholesale middlemen, we deliver raw, unadulterated saffron directly from our farm corms to kitchens worldwide.

Every stigma is picked by hand at sunrise, shade-dried on traditional mesh screens, and preserved in violet glass. This ensures that you receive the highest concentrations of color (color), flavor (Picrocolor), and aroma (Safranal) possible.

Geographical Superiority

Why Pampore Saffron is Legendary

  • Unique Microclimate: Snowy cold winters and dry, warm autumns stimulate heavy essential oils in the corms.
  • High color Strength: Altitude and organic clay soil combine to yield color levels above 220, beating global standards.
  • Traditional Cultivation: We manual weed our fields with wooden hand hoes, using no commercial pesticide sprays.
  • Global Reputation: Pampore's Karewa plateaus have produced the world's most sought-after culinary and wellness saffron for centuries.

How It's Made

From Cultivation to Supply

Five steps of manual craftsmanship behind every package of KesarHarvesters saffron.

1

Soil Cultivation

Manual weeding twice a year using traditional hand tools, protecting bulbs from synthetic sprays.

2

Flower Picking & Separation

Blossoms hand-picked at dawn. Red stigmas separated from yellow styles by family artisans.

3

Traditional Curing

Shade-dried slowly on mesh screens for 72 hours, locking in essential safranal oils.

4

Quality Lock Packaging

Checked for color index, then sealed in airtight violet glass vials to prevent visible light damage.

5

Direct Supply

Shipped direct to home kitchens across India via premium courier partners with live tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your saffron authentic?

Yes. Every single filament is sourced in Namblabal, Pampore, J&K. We print our FSSAI credentials on the back label and support simple water purity tests at home.

How is it shipped?

We use major domestic shipping aggregators to dispatch orders within 24 hours of checkout. Tracked parcels are delivered across India in 3 to 5 business days.

How should saffron be stored?

Keep the saffron inside the provided violet glass jar. Store it in a cool, dark cupboard. Do not freeze or refrigerate, as condensation will damp and spoil the threads.

Do you offer wholesale orders?

Yes. We supply B2B packaging and bulk saffron starting from 100g. You can submit the form on our Wholesale page to receive custom pricing.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We ship to all PIN codes in India. A flat delivery charge of ₹99 applies per parcel.
