Privacy Policy

Official terms, guidelines, and policies of KesarHarvesters Saffron brand.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on our website or contact us, we collect the customer details required to process your transaction and deliver the product. This includes your Full Name, Shipping Address, WhatsApp/Phone Number, and Email Address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your contact and delivery details to:

  • Verify and fulfill your orders.
  • Generate and share UPI payment QR codes or links.
  • Send order confirmation, tracking updates, and delivery alerts via WhatsApp/SMS or Email.
  • Respond to your wholesale B2B and corporate gifting queries.

3. Data Integrity & Payment Protection

Because we do not capture credit card details on our servers (payments are handled securely via third-party UPI gateways like GPay, PhonePe, or PayTM to our ID 8825034663@yescred), your financial transaction details remain completely private and secure with your bank.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share delivery details with our trusted domestic logistics partners (India Post Speed Post, Blue Dart, Delhivery) to facilitate shipping.
