Bulk Supply Guidelines

Direct Farm Sourcing Terms

We support bulk purchases starting at **100 Grams** for domestic retailers, luxury hotels, organic spice packers, and wedding planners.

For international distributors, we provide formal commercial invoices, lab reports verifying Premium Quality standards, and support phytosanitary inspections. Export orders are cleared from customs in India via DGFT guidelines.

Custom retail packaging (private label vial packing with your custom brand sticker) is available for orders above 1 Kilogram.

GSTIN certificate and business credentials are mandatory to receive B2B tax invoice pricing.
